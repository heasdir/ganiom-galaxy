# --- MISSION 1: TROUBLE IN FOREN SPACE (INTRO) ---
mission "Foren Campaign 1: Intro"
	name "Trouble in Foren Space"
	landing
	invisible
	source
		government "The Forens"
	to offer
		has "SF : A Foren Delivery: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A Foren approaches you urgently. Your translator activates automatically this time.`
			`"Human. You have delivered for us before. We need your help again, but this time it is not cargo."`
			`"Hisair forces have been attacking our planets. Fo'kak'ni'si'slok was hit yesterday. They are getting bolder."`
			choice
				`	"What do you need from me?"`
					goto task
				`	"Why is Hisair attacking you?"`
					goto reason
			label reason
			`	"Resources. Territory. Hisair wants what we have. They have always been a problem for this galaxy. The diplomatic council says never trust a Hisair. We learned this the hard way."`
				goto task
			label task
			`	"We need someone to defend our planets. Our ships are stretched thin. Will you help?"`
			choice
				`	"I'll defend your planets."`
					goto accept
				`	"I need more time."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"We are grateful, human. Fo'kak'ni'si'slok needs you first. Move quickly."`
				accept
	on accept
		"foren c" ++
