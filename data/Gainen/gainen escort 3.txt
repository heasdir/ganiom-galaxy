# Gainen Campaign - Escort Missions
# Escorts Clossusn ships to key Gainen Republic planets
# Final mission escorts to Reisbruth planet Hsahi


mission "Gainen Escort 1"
	name "Escort to Gasinas"
	landing
	description "Escort the Gainen Republic Clossusn to Gasinas in the Gainensiti system. Do not let it be destroyed."
	source
		government "Gainen Republic"
	destination "Gasinas"
	to offer
		has "Gainen Hisair 4: Northpoint : done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A Gainen Republic officer approaches you at the spaceport.`
			`"Human. We have a Clossusn carrying vital supplies and personnel to Gainensiti. Communist Ganiom forces have been spotted in the area. We need an escort."`
			choice
				`	"I'll escort it."`
					goto accept
				`	"What's a Clossusn?"`
					goto explain
			label explain
			`	"One of our finest carriers. Built during the war against Communist Ganiom. It carries seven light warship bays and four medium warship bays. It is not defenseless, but against a determined Communist Ganiom force it needs support."`
			choice
				`	"I'll do it."`
					goto accept
				`	"I'm not ready for this."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"Good. The Clossusn will depart when you are ready. Do not let it be destroyed."`
				accept
	npc escort
		government "Gainen Republic"
		personality escort heroic
		fleet
			names "Gainen Military"
			variant
				"Clossusn"
	npc kill
		government "Communist Ganiom"
		personality heroic
		fleet
			names "Commune Security"
			variant
				Dreadnought 2
				Aerie 4
	on visit
		dialog `You have arrived at Gasinas but have not landed. Land to complete the escort.`
	on complete
		payment 8000000
		"reputation: Gainen Republic" += 15
		"gainen escort" ++
		dialog `The Clossusn lands safely at Gasinas. The officer contacts you. "Well done. The supplies have arrived safely. Here is <payment>. We have more escorts that need protection."`

mission "Gainen Escort 4: Reisbruth"
	name "Interfactional Escort to Hsahi"
	landing
	description "Escort the Gainen Republic Clossusn to Hsahi in Reisbruth space. This is an interfactional mission of great importance."
	source
		government "Gainen Republic"
	destination "Hsahi"
	to offer
		has "Gainen Escort 3: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`The senior officer meets you again, this time accompanied by what appears to be a Reisbruth diplomat.`
			`"Human. You know of our ancient partnership with Reisbruth. This escort is different. We are sending a Clossusn to Hsahi in Reisbruth space carrying diplomatic personnel and technology for our joint development agreement."`
			`The Reisbruth diplomat steps forward. "REGAZAMANI TCHULIAK. You have proven yourself to Gainen Republic. Reisbruth acknowledges your service. Escort this ship safely and Reisbruth will consider you a known ally."`
			choice
				`	"It would be an honor."`
					goto accept
				`	"Will Reisbruth space be safe?"`
					goto safe
			label safe
			`	The Reisbruth diplomat almost looks offended. "Reisbruth space is always safe. Unless you are 100kg of nitrogen." He says this completely seriously.`

				`	"Understood. I'll escort them."`
					goto accept
				`	"I need more time."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"REISBRUTH SHAIRMAAAAAAAAAAAAA," the diplomat says quietly, nodding with great dignity.`
				accept
