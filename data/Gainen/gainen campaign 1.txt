mission "First Contact: Gainen Republic"
	landing
	to offer
		not "fist contact: gainen"
		not system "Isa Palechunam"
	source
		government "Gainen Republic"
	on offer
		conversation
			"As you land, the atmosphere of the starport feels heavy. A group of Gainen officials approaches your vessel."
			"The translator crackles: Being from beyond the Wormhole link, you have entered the territory of the Gainen Republic. Identify your intent. Are you a scout, or an isolated traveler?"
			choice
				"I am a traveler from a distant galaxy called the Milky Way."
					goto `milky_way`
			choice
				"I am just exploring. I found the wormhole by accident."
					goto "accident"

			label "milky_way"
				"Milky Way? Unknown. But if you come in peace, Gainisen will permit your presence."
				goto `next_step"

			label "accident"
				"A dangerous accident. The currents of the Wormhole link to the core have claimed many Gainen pilots. You are either very skilled or very lucky."
				goto "next_step"

			label "next_step"
			"The official narrows his gaze. Whatever your origin, you are here now. But you look... familiar. Too familiar."
			choice
				"What do you mean?"
					goto "explain_familiar"
			choice
				"I am just a pilot."
					goto "just_pilot"

			label `explain_familiar`
				"The humans. They look like you. Go to Isa Palechunam in Makferuiko. Our security forces there will determine if you are a friend or a target."
				goto `contact_end`

			label "just_pilot"
				"Perhaps. But security is not a matter of -perhaps-. Go to Isa Palechunam for screening. Now."
				goto `contact_end`

			label "contact_end"
				accept
	on complete
		set "fist contact: gainen"

mission `Gainen: The Spy Accusation`
	landing
	invisible
	to offer
		has `fist contact: gainen`
		not `gainen: cleared of spying`
	source
		system `Isa Palechunam`
	on offer
		conversation
			`Security forces surround your ship. Halt! You have the face of a Ganiom! And a blue ship? Are you a bloody spy or not?`
			choice
				` I am not saying a single word.`
					goto `spy_silence`
			choice
				` You mean those sick bastards who did that to Siri Om? I am nothing like them!`
					goto `spy_praise`

			label `spy_silence`
				`Silence! Only a Magenta rat hides behind a wall of quiet! If you will not cooperate, we will treat you as an enemy of the Republic!`
				on accept
					`reputation: Gainen Republic` -= 500
				goto `interrogation_phase_2`

			label `spy_praise`
				`You know of Siri Om? The Ganiom claim it was a -liberation.- You speak with the fire of someone who has felt the Magenta sting.`
				on accept
					`reputation: Gainen Republic` += 100
					`reputation: Communist Ganiom` -= 1000
				goto `interrogation_phase_2`

			label `interrogation_phase_2`
			`The official narrows his eye-stalks. Even if you hate them, you could be a double agent. Why should we trust a human flying a blue ship?`
			choice
				` Because I want to make money, and the Ganiom are broke.`
					goto `mercenary`
			choice
				` Because I will help you wipe them out.`
					goto `warrior`

			label `mercenary`
				`A mercenary heart... at least that is honest. The Republic is rich enough to buy your loyalty.`
				goto `spy_conclusion`

			label `warrior`
				`Bold words. We shall see if your cannons match your tongue.`
				goto `spy_conclusion`

			label `spy_conclusion`
				`Go to our capital planet, Gainisen. If you want to prove you are not Magenta, we have work for you.`
				accept
	on complete
		set `gainen: cleared of spying`
