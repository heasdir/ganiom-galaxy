mission "Sudden Appearance"
	name "Sudden Appearance"
	landing
	invisible
	source
		government "Gainen Republic"
	on offer
		conversation
			`The Gainen you encounter look unusually tall with orange skin. A Gainen authority officer approaches you before you can leave the landing pad.`
			`"You are not Serbajkil. You are not Foren. You are human. Are you a Communist Ganiom spy?"`
			choice
				`	"I'm only travelling. I mean no harm."`
					goto travelling
				`	"Communist Ganiom? I don't even know what that is."`
					goto confused
			label confused
			`	The officer narrows their eyes. "Convenient answer. Communist Ganiom are humans, just like you, and they have caused us much grief. If you are not one of them, prove it."`
				goto task
			label travelling
			`	"Travellers don't usually end up this far from the Milky Way," the officer says. "You're either very lost, very brave, or a Communist Ganiom agent. Which is it?"`
			choice
				`	"Very lost, honestly."`
					goto task
				`	"Very brave."`
					goto task
			label task
			`	The officer sighs. "Either way, you can prove your intentions. Communist Ganiom forces have been raiding our systems. Help us repel them and we will consider you an ally."`
			choice
				`	"I'll help."`
					goto accept
				`	"I don't have a strong enough ship yet."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"Good. We will contact you when they strike next."`
				accept
	on accept
		"reputation: Communist Ganiom" <?= -10
		"gainen c" ++
