# Gainen Campaign Part 2 - Hisair Arc
# Starts at Isa Palechunam, ends at Northpoint Station


mission "Gainen Hisair 1"
	name "The Hisair Problem"
	landing
	invisible
	source
		planet "Isa Palechunam"
	to offer
		has "Defend Gainen 3: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`A high ranking Gainen Republic intelligence officer approaches you. She looks serious.`
			`"Human. You have proven yourself against Communist Ganiom. But there is a bigger problem. Hisair."`
			`"They have been supplying Communist Ganiom with weapons and resources. Without Hisair support, Communist Ganiom would collapse within weeks."`
			choice
				`	"What do you need me to do?"`
					goto task
				`	"Why not deal with Hisair directly?"`
					goto explain
			label explain
			`	"We cannot. Hisair controls the Oganesson supply in this region. Without it, our ships cannot function. We must be careful. Very careful."`
				goto task
			label task
			`	"We need you to intercept a Hisair supply convoy heading to Communist Ganiom territory. Destroy it. Do not let that cargo reach them."`
			choice
				`	"Consider it done."`
					goto accept
				`	"I need more time."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"Good. The convoy is marked on your map. Move quickly."`
				accept
	on accept
		"gainen hisair" ++

mission "Gainen Hisair 3"
	name "The Truth About Hisair"
	landing
	invisible
	source
		planet "Isa Palechunam"
	to offer
		has "Gainen Hisair 2: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`The intelligence officer pulls you aside quietly. She looks troubled.`
			`"I have discovered something. Something that changes everything."`
			`"The Sini'Om massacre. We blamed Communist Ganiom. But our intelligence suggests it was Hisair. They used Communist Ganiom logos as a false flag."`
			choice
				`	"Are you certain?"`
					goto certain
				`	"Communist Ganiom has been innocent this whole time?"`
					goto innocent
			label certain
			`	"Not yet. But the evidence is mounting. I need you to retrieve a data core from a Hisair outpost. It may contain proof."`
				goto task
			label innocent
			`	"Of the massacre, yes. They are still our enemies in other ways. But the Sini'Om... that was Hisair."`
				goto task
			label task
			`	"There is a Hisair outpost in Fornaikao. Get in, retrieve the data core, get out. Do not engage unless necessary."`
			choice
				`	"I will get the data."`
					goto accept
				`	"This is too dangerous."`
					decline
			label accept
			`	"Be careful. Hisair does not take kindly to intruders."`
				accept
	on accept
		"gainen hisair" ++
